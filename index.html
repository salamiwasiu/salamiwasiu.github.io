How I work

The same five steps for a grant or a tender.

Fit check

You send the funding call or tender and your deadline. I confirm eligibility and what's needed.

Mapping

Every requirement, scoring criterion, word limit and mandatory document, listed.

Evidence

A short questionnaire so every answer uses your real experience.

Drafting

I write, you review, I refine.

Final check

A submission-ready pack with a compliance checklist.

Compliant is not the same as competitive. A compliant answer follows the instructions; a competitive one makes it easy to award you marks. I write for both, and I'll tell you if a grant or tender isn't a good fit before you spend money on it.
